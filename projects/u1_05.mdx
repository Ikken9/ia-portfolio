Customer segmentation represents a fundamental application of unsupervised learning in business analytics. This
implementation demonstrates a complete pipeline from data exploration to customer clustering using K-Means and PCA on
real mall customer data.

## Dataset Overview

The Mall Customer Segmentation Dataset contains demographic and behavioral data from 200 customers, providing insights
into shopping patterns and customer characteristics.

**Sample Data:**
| Customer ID | Genre | Age | Annual Income (k$) | Spending Score |
|:-----------:|:-----:|:---:|:------------------:|:--------------:|
| 1           | Male  | 19  | 15                 | 39             |
| 2           | Male  | 21  | 15                 | 81             |
| 3           | Female| 20  | 16                 | 6              |

**Dataset Statistics:**
- **Gender Distribution:** Female 56.0%, Male 44.0%
- **Age Range:** 18-70 years (mean: 38.9)
- **Annual Income:** $15k-$137k (mean: $60.6k)
- **Spending Score:** 1-99 (mean: 50.2)

Three scalers evaluated for data normalization:

MinMaxScaler demonstrated superior performance and was selected for final implementation.

## Feature Selection Comparison

**Methods Comparison:**
<MDXImage src="/u1_05_8.png" alt="feature selection methods comparison" />

Four approaches evaluated for clustering preparation:
- **Baseline (all features):** Silhouette Score = 0.364
- **Forward Selection:** Silhouette Score = 0.573
- **Backward Elimination:** Silhouette Score = 0.573
- **PCA (2D):** Silhouette Score = 0.686

PCA achieved the highest performance and was selected for final clustering.

## Optimal Cluster Determination

**Elbow Method & Silhouette Analysis:**
<MDXImage src="/u1_05_9.png" alt="cluster optimization" />

- Elbow Method suggested K=6
- Silhouette Analysis suggested K=2 (score=0.762)
- Business context consideration led to selection of K=4 clusters
